# Flags for a human

Everything the team needs a person to supply, confirm or decide. Teammates add entries; the launch strategist groups them and removes duplicates. Only a human ticks an entry off.

Format: `- [ ] Question or gap. (raised by: teammate, file)`

## Blocking: answer before Phase 1 if you can

- [ ] What is the launch date? Are there dates to avoid? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] What is the overall budget range for paid media, and any for PR tools or an agency? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] What is the link for the "Get started free" button? The brief's calls-to-action table leaves it blank. (raised by: set-up, docs/embed-brief.md)
- [ ] When should each call to action be used? The brief's "When" column is empty. (raised by: set-up, docs/embed-brief.md)
- [ ] The brief refers to an "open questions" section, but none is included. Is there one? (raised by: set-up, docs/embed-brief.md)
- [ ] What are the target markets: UK only, or UK plus others? This affects press, ad targeting and timing. (raised by: set-up)

## People, quotes and story

- [ ] Who are the spokespeople? Give names, roles and short bios. (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] What is the founder's story: why Learnt.ai built Embed? This is for the Product Hunt maker comment, the press release and founder posts. (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Which approved quotes can the press release use? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Which facts about Learnt.ai (founded, location, team size, funding) can be used in "About Learnt.ai"? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Who is the media contact (name, role, email)? (raised by: set-up)

## Assets

- [ ] Brand guidelines, logo files, colours and fonts. (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Real product screenshots and access to record a demo video. (raised by: set-up)

## Accounts, access and decisions

- [ ] Which ad accounts exist (LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Google Ads), and who sets up campaigns? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Which analytics tool is used, and which sign-up and activation events can it track? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Who are the Product Hunt maker and team accounts? Should we use a hunter? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Is there an existing email list, and do you have consent to email it about the launch? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Should the ads bid on or target competitor brand names? The default is no. (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Should one publication get an exclusive or embargoed early look? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] Who covers replies on Product Hunt, in communities and on social on launch day? (raised by: set-up)
- [ ] What are the KPI targets for sign-ups, teams going live and cost per sign-up? (raised by: set-up)
