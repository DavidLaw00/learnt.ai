## Why this matters for conversion

Waalaxy visits a profile and sends an invitation; the prospect opens **your** profile before they accept or reply. So the profile is the landing page for every outbound experiment. It has to answer three questions in seconds:

1. **Who is this?** The headline.
2. **Why should I care?** The first two lines of About, before "see more".
3. **What do I do next?** One action, everywhere: book a demo before launch, get started free from launch.
